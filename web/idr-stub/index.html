What is an IDR?

An Intent Decision Record is the smallest unit of audited delegation. Every time DONNA hands work to a person or a model, one IDR is signed and chained to the previous one.

The shape

An IDR is a small structured object — a few fields, no surprises:

example IDR 
{
  "intent":         "Review clause 7 of the partnership agreement.",
  "signer":         "associate.partner@firm.example",
  "model":          "groq/llama-3.3-70b-versatile",
  "confidence":     0.82,
  "evidence_refs":  ["doc/agreement_v3.pdf#p7"],
  "previous_hash":  "9f1c…3b",
  "ts":             "2026-05-11T18:30:00Z",
  "signature":      "HMAC-SHA256(…)"
}

Why it matters

Most "AI for legal" leaves you with a chat log. A chat log is not evidence. An IDR is.

Because each record is hash-chained to the last, you can replay the entire delegation history of a matter and prove that nothing was rewritten after the fact. The verifier is stdlib Python — no proprietary tooling, no vendor lock-in.
