What the letters mean.

DDecision

The unit of work. Every delegated action emits a structured Intent Decision Record — who decided, on what evidence, with what confidence — captured at the moment.

OOriented

Architecture orients around Decisions, not documents and not chats. Decisions are the first-class object.

NNetwork

Two networks: language models routed across providers, and attorneys + matters with delegations that persist across firms.

NNotarisation

Every delegated decision is signed and chained to the prior one. The chain replays for audit, regulators, partners. Tamper-evident by construction.

A(for) Attorneys

Legal vertical, exactly. Attorneys are both the audience DONNA serves and the partners who shape what it becomes.

60 seconds · what DONNA does

From spoken intent to a verifiable trail.

  1. You speak. "Get the partnership agreement reviewed for clause 7 ambiguity by Friday."
  2. DONNA captures it. Voice → text → structured intent. Sensitive material is redacted before any model sees it.
  3. DONNA routes it. The right human or AI gets the job, with the brief and the deadline. Free public tier uses an open language model — no paid keys required to try.
  4. The work returns. Output comes back with a confidence score and a falsification check.
  5. It's notarised. A signed Intent Decision Record is hash-chained to the previous one. Re-check the chain in seconds, on any machine, with stdlib tools.
